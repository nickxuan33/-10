Replace each section with what you found during your own testing. Keep facts
specific and dated, and describe the product rather than promotions.

## Signing up and verification

How long did sign-up take? Which age, identity and location checks did you go
through, and did anything go wrong?

## Games

Which games did you play on desktop and mobile? Note the range of slots, table
games and live dealer tables, and anything missing.

## Deposits and withdrawals

Which payment methods did you use? Record the date you requested a withdrawal
and the date the money arrived.

## Mobile experience

Did you use an app or the mobile site? How fast and easy was it?

## Customer support

Which channels did you try, and how quickly and clearly did support answer?

## Responsible gambling tools

Where are deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion in the account
settings, and how easy were they to set up?

## Verdict

Summarize who the site suits, and explain your rating.
