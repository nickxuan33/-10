{{ site.site_name }} is free to read. We are funded by affiliate commissions: when you open an account with an operator through a link on our site, the operator may pay us a fee. It costs you nothing extra.

## How we keep our reviews independent

- Operators can't pay for a better rating or a better position on our pages.
- We only link to operators on the Alberta iGaming Corporation's approved list.
- Our ratings come from our own testing. See [how we review](/how-we-review/).

## How to recognize our affiliate links

Buttons such as "Visit" on our review pages are affiliate links. They go through addresses that start with /go/, and we mark them as sponsored links for search engines.
