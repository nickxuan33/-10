This policy explains how {{ site.site_name }} ("we") handles information when you visit our website.

## Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you.

## Links to operators

When you follow a link to an operator, the operator's own privacy policy applies. Operators and affiliate networks may use cookies or similar technology to record that you arrived from our site.

## Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete your emails, by writing to [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}).

## Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top.
