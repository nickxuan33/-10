Every review on {{ site.site_name }} follows the same process, so you can compare sites fairly and see exactly what our ratings mean.

## 1. Only approved operators qualify

We review a site only if it appears on the Alberta iGaming Corporation's [list of approved iGaming sites](https://www.abigaming.ca/players/approved-igaming-sites). Each review shows the date we last checked that list. We check it again at least once a month and remove any site that is no longer listed.

## 2. We test with a real account

For each site, we:

- sign up and complete the age, identity and location checks;
- make a deposit and a withdrawal with our own money, and record how long the withdrawal takes;
- play a range of games on a computer and on a phone;
- contact customer support with real questions;
- set up the responsible gambling tools, such as deposit limits and time-outs.

## 3. What we rate

| Area | What we look at |
| --- | --- |
| Approval and trust | Approval status, clear terms and company information |
| Games | Range and quality of slots, table games and live dealer games |
| Payments | Methods available in Alberta, fees and withdrawal speed |
| Mobile | App or mobile site, speed and ease of use |
| Support | Channels, hours and quality of answers |
| Responsible gambling | Limits, time-outs and self-exclusion, and how easy they are to find |

Our overall rating out of 5 is the editor's judgement across these areas, and every review explains it.

## 4. What we don't do

- **No bonus listings.** Alberta's advertising rules restrict how bonuses and other inducements can be promoted, and they say little about the quality of a site. We focus on the product.
- **No paid ratings.** Operators can't pay to change a rating or a position on our pages.

## 5. Updates and corrections

We update a review when something important changes, such as payment options or approval status, and show the date of the latest update. If you spot a mistake, please [contact us](/contact/).
