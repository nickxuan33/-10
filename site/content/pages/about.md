{{ site.site_name }} is an independent website for players in Alberta. We review online casinos that are approved to operate in the province, and we publish plain-English guides to the rules, the odds and responsible gambling.

## What we stand for

- **Approved sites only.** We only cover operators on the Alberta iGaming Corporation's approved list, and we check that list again at least once a month.
- **Our own testing.** Our reviews are based on our own accounts and hands-on testing. See [how we review](/how-we-review/).
- **Straight talk about risk.** Casino games favour the casino, and we never present gambling as a solution to money problems.
- **Open about how we're funded.** We may earn a commission when readers sign up through our links. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## We are not the regulator

We are not a gambling operator, and we are not affiliated with Alberta Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis (AGLC) or the Alberta iGaming Corporation (AiGC). For official information, visit the [Alberta iGaming Corporation](https://www.abigaming.ca/players/) or [AGLC](https://aglc.ca/).
