Gambling should be a form of entertainment that you can afford. It is not a way to make money: every casino game favours the casino over time, so only gamble with money you are prepared to lose.

## Get help now

If gambling is causing problems for you or someone close to you, free and confidential help is available in Alberta:

- **Addiction Helpline (Alberta Health Services):** call [1-866-332-2322](tel:+18663322322), 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can also dial 811.
- **211 Alberta:** dial 211 to be connected to community and social services near you.
- **GameSense by AGLC:** information, support and the self-exclusion program at [gamesenseab.ca](https://gamesenseab.ca/).
- **Gamblers Anonymous:** peer support meetings, listed on the [Gamblers Anonymous website](https://www.gamblersanonymous.org/).

## Tips for staying in control

- Set a budget before you play and stop when it's spent.
- Set a time limit and take regular breaks.
- Never chase losses by betting more to get your money back.
- Don't gamble when you are stressed, feeling low or drinking.
- Don't borrow money to gamble, and never treat gambling as an investment or a way to pay bills.

## Tools at approved sites

Sites on the Alberta iGaming Corporation's approved list offer responsible gambling tools. Look in your account settings for options such as deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion, and set them up before you play.

## Self-exclusion

Alberta has a province-wide self-exclusion program that lets you exclude yourself from gambling for a period you choose. Learn how it works on [GameSense's self-exclusion page](https://gamesenseab.ca/responsible-gambling-support/self-exclusion-program), or ask the site you play on.

## Warning signs

Consider getting help if you:

- spend more money or time gambling than you planned;
- need to bet more to feel the same excitement;
- chase losses or borrow money to gamble;
- hide your gambling from people close to you;
- feel anxious, irritable or low when you try to cut back.

## Age limit

You must be 18 or older to gamble in Alberta. If you share a device with young people, use parental controls and keep your gambling accounts logged out.
