## Corrections

If something on our site is wrong or out of date, tell us which page and what needs to change. We check every report and update the page if needed.

## Problems with an operator

We can't resolve disputes with operators. Contact the operator's customer support first. If that doesn't solve the problem, approved operators in Alberta are covered by the Alberta iGaming Corporation's complaints process: see [AiGC's information for players](https://www.abigaming.ca/players/).

## Help with gambling

If you need support, call the Addiction Helpline at [1-866-332-2322](tel:+18663322322), 24 hours a day, or see our [responsible gambling page](/responsible-gambling/).
