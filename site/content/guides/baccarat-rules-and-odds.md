Baccarat is one of the simplest casino games: you choose a bet, and the dealer follows fixed rules for everything else. This guide covers the standard game, often called punto banco, which is the version usually offered online and at live dealer tables.

## The goal

Two hands are dealt: the **Player** hand and the **Banker** hand. You don't play either hand yourself. Instead, you bet on which hand will finish closer to 9, or that the two will tie.

## Card values

- Aces count as 1.
- Cards 2 to 9 count at face value.
- 10s, jacks, queens and kings count as 0.

A hand is worth the last digit of the sum of its cards. For example, 7 + 8 = 15, so the hand is worth 5. The highest possible total is 9.

## How a round is played

1. The Player and Banker hands each get two cards.
2. If either hand totals 8 or 9 (a "natural"), both hands stand and the round is settled.
3. Otherwise, the Player hand may take a third card, then the Banker hand may take one, following the rules below.

### Player's third card

| Player's first two cards | Player |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 5 | Draws a third card |
| 6 or 7 | Stands |
| 8 or 9 | Natural: no more cards |

### Banker's third card

If the Player stood on 6 or 7, the Banker draws on 0 to 5 and stands on 6 or 7. If the Player drew a third card, the Banker follows this table:

| Banker's total | Banker draws when the Player's third card is |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 | Any card |
| 3 | Anything except an 8 |
| 4 | 2 to 7 |
| 5 | 4 to 7 |
| 6 | 6 or 7 |
| 7 | Never: the Banker stands |

The dealer applies these rules automatically, so you don't need to memorize them to play.

## Bets, payouts and house edge

| Bet | Pays | House edge (8 decks) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banker | 1 to 1, minus 5% commission | 1.06% |
| Player | 1 to 1 | 1.24% |
| Tie | 8 to 1 | 14.36% |

When the hands tie, Banker and Player bets are returned. Some tables pay the Tie bet at 9 to 1, which lowers its house edge to about 4.84%. Side bets usually carry a higher house edge than the main bets, so check the paytable before you place one.

The Banker bet has the lowest house edge, but every bet in baccarat favours the casino over time. Our [house edge guide](/guides/house-edge-explained/) explains what these numbers mean for your budget.

## Common myths

- **"Streaks can be predicted."** Scorecards and trend charts only show what already happened. Past hands tell you almost nothing about the next one.
- **"A betting system can beat baccarat."** Changing the size of your bets doesn't change the house edge on each bet.
- **"The Banker bet always wins."** It wins slightly more often than the Player bet, which is why the casino takes a commission on it.
