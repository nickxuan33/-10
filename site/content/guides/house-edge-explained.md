Every casino game is designed so that, over time, the casino keeps a share of the money wagered. That share is called the **house edge**. Understanding it is the best protection against unrealistic expectations.

## What house edge means

The house edge is the average percentage of each bet that the casino expects to keep over the long run. With a house edge of 2%, you should expect to lose about $2 for every $100 you wager, on average.

In a single session you may win or lose far more than that, because short-term results swing a lot. The more you play, the closer your overall result tends to get to the house edge.

## House edge and RTP

Slots and many online games describe the same idea as **return to player (RTP)**: the share of wagered money that a game pays back over the long run. RTP and house edge add up to 100%, so a slot with 96% RTP has a house edge of 4%.

## House edge in popular games

| Game and bet | House edge |
| --- | --- |
| Baccarat: Banker bet (5% commission) | 1.06% |
| Baccarat: Player bet | 1.24% |
| Baccarat: Tie bet (pays 8 to 1) | 14.36% |
| European roulette (single zero) | 2.70% |
| American roulette (double zero) | 5.26% |
| Blackjack | Depends on the table rules and your decisions |
| Slots | Set by each game; see its RTP |

The baccarat figures assume an eight-deck shoe. In blackjack, the edge depends on details such as the number of decks and what a blackjack pays, and it rises quickly if you make poor decisions. For slots, look for the RTP in the game's information or help screen.

## Why betting systems don't beat the edge

Systems such as the Martingale, where you double your stake after every loss, change the pattern of your wins and losses but not the house edge. Each bet still carries the same edge, and a run of losses can quickly reach the table limit or the end of your budget. No sequence of bets turns a game that favours the casino into one that favours you.

## What this means when you play

- Treat the house edge as the price of your entertainment, not a hurdle to beat.
- Decide on a budget you can afford to lose before you play, and stop when it's gone.
- Games with a lower edge make a budget last longer on average, but they still favour the casino.
